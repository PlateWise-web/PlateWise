Calorie counter

Enter your sex, age, height, weight, activity and goal to get a daily calorie range, then fine-tune it yourself.

7-day meal plan

Breakfast, lunch, snack and dinner, 2 to 6 meals a day, built so every day lands inside your calorie range.

Smart grocery list

Ingredients added up across the week, grouped by category and scaled to your household.

Budget tracking

Choose your country and currency, set a weekly budget and watch the cost bar. Edit any price to match your shop.

Local price check

Prices estimated and double-checked for your country every time you generate a week.

Five diet styles

Allergy filters

Favourites and your own meals

One-tap swaps

How-to-prepare guide
